# v0.4 preset library and lab comparison

## Implemented

- Added versioned `horizontal_drift@0.4.0` and `cinematic_float@0.4.0` alongside the compatible `slow_push@0.3.0`.
- Added `subtle`, `standard`, and `strong` intensity defaults. Scene evaluation uses only the integer frame index, fixed FPS, immutable scene values, and a 32-bit seed.
- Applied preset-specific limits to push, lateral travel, roll, and depth strength. The resolver raises overscan when a valid combination needs more crop room and reports every clamp.
- Added depth-gradient damping at sharp boundaries for the two new presets. The v0.3 slow push shader path keeps damping at zero to preserve its output.
- Added preset, intensity, and seed controls to the lab. The resolved scene panel displays renderer and preset versions plus each evaluated camera transform.
- The lab generates three midpoint gallery tiles per corpus entry using one prepared source/depth pair and a snapshot of intensity and seed.

## Verification

- `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm test` pass with Node 22.23.1: 29 unit, 4 integration, and 12 depth tests.
- `pnpm test:browser:depth` passes in Chromium, including a visible lateral displacement check for `horizontal_drift`.
- A local 43-image candidate corpus produced 129/129 midpoint gallery tiles with no browser page errors. Clicking a tile selected its preset, and changing intensity or seed recomputed the scene.
- First, middle, and final frames were captured for four representative illustrated, portrait, architecture, and difficult-edge scenes. Local ignored review sheets are in `benchmarks/gallery/candidate-v0.4/`.
- Pure-function tests cover exact endpoints, repeatability at a fixed seed, seed variation for cinematic float, frame-by-frame safety limits, preset-specific clamps, and overscan adjustment.

## Remaining release evidence

The candidate images are not the approved frozen corpus. The v0.3 core-effect review and human corpus approval remain open. The v0.4 motion-variety decision and full corpus comparison are therefore provisional; the illustrated diagrams in particular need the v0.5 risk and 2D fallback path. No private media is committed to this branch.
