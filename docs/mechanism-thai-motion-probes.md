# Thai motion and tracking probes

The preflight command saves the six exact research stress strings and the hashed
SS02 production phrase in 128 validated authoring scenes and native compositions:

```sh
node --import tsx tests/browser/mechanism-thai-motion-preflight.ts
```

Each text pairs zero and opt-in optical tracking with intact motion, independent
translation, rotation, opacity, retyping, and color/size/font-asset boundaries.
At 96 px optical tracking resolves to −25 per 1,000 em (−2.4 px); ordinary defaults
and explicit zero overrides stay at zero. Seven additional controls for each of
those latter modes verify identical zero-tracking geometry and poses.

The pinned Noto bytes, real axes and exact-copy coverage are checked before loaded
browser shaping and raster preparation. Every case saves its source, composition,
layout, run styles and 24 frames of poses. Intact control authoring uses full
selection, zero stagger, an all-text anchor, no mask and explicit
`excludeSpaces: false`. Spans use grapheme indices; an interior combining-mark
UTF-16 offset is not an admissible span boundary under the existing contract.

Font-asset boundaries use an alias of the same exact Noto bytes. This tests the
asset/run boundary without claiming support for distinct Thai faces. Two mixed
Latin controls assign only `X2` to real pinned Plex bytes. Seven Plex-only Thai
coverage negatives retain the missing-codepoint diagnostics.

[Preflight evidence](./mechanism-shorts-ms2-thai-preflight-results.json) retains
both failed authoring attempts and the independently audited final matrix.
Preflight does not establish native encoded appearance or safe finer Thai motion.
The saved compositions still require consecutive encoded inspection of active and
resolved states, attached marks, overlap, gaps and complete meaning at master and
declared 320/360 LANCZOS bitmap reductions. Retyping particularly requires active
transition inspection beyond the saved normal text poses. No default tracking or
rendering behavior has changed, and no human acceptance is inferred.
