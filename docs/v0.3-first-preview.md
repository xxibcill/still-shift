# v0.3 first animated preview

## Implemented

- Shared renderer core resolves versioned `slow_push@0.3.0` scenes and evaluates a
  frame from its integer index and fixed 30 FPS timeline.
- WebGL2 preview maps the source and prepared depth texture onto a subdivided plane.
  Non-finite depth samples are neutralized in the shader; the source/depth pair and
  timeline are validated before drawing.
- Cover fit and at least 10% overscan keep the subtle push inside the crop envelope.
  Travel and depth strength have conservative hard limits and report clamps.
- Browser lab displays the source, depth texture, resolved scene, frame parameters,
  and a scrub/play preview. It can prepare each checksum-verified corpus entry and
  build a midpoint preview gallery. It also accepts a local prepared pair.

## Verification

- `tests/unit/renderer-core.test.ts` checks deterministic endpoints, all-frame
  safety bounds, aspect-ratio cover fit, and bad inputs. The shader guards invalid
  depth samples before displacement.
- `pnpm test:browser:depth` renders synthetic near and far markers in Chromium and
  checks that the near marker moves farther between the first and final frames.
- A browser smoke test loaded a PNG through the v0.2 fake depth adapter, then loaded
  its normalized `source.png` and `depth.png` in the lab. Chromium rendered frame 0
  and frame 149 without a page error. A sampled preview pixel changed from
  `[70, 120, 185, 255]` at frame 0 to `[71, 120, 184, 255]` at frame 149.
- A synthetic one-image corpus exercised checksum verification, fake depth preparation,
  gallery tile generation, and reopening the tile for animation review in Chromium.
- The v0.1 no-op CLI is still the animation-engine path; preview does not claim MP4
  export.

## Candidate corpus technical review (2026-09-24)

A separate, untracked candidate manifest assembled 43 existing explainer-workflow
stills from two local projects: 28 illustrated episode shots and 15 photographic
commercial storyboard shots. The manifest records source checksums, dimensions,
private evaluation rights notes, and all seven required categories. It remains
`incomplete` because the required human selection and rights review is pending.
No private source image or absolute source path is committed to this repository.

The pinned Depth Anything V2 Small adapter prepared all 43 candidates on Apple MPS
with zero failures. Every source was a cache miss; median preparation time was
979 ms and the first inference took 31.3 seconds including model startup. The lab
then rendered first and final `slow_push` frames for all 43 without a browser page
error. Local, ignored artifacts are under `benchmarks/gallery/candidate-v0.3/` and
`benchmarks/results/candidate-v0.3-*.json`.

Reviewing all endpoint pairs found no obvious exposed borders or gross geometry
breaks at this size. The photographic scenes showed useful depth separation;
flat illustrated diagrams often produced depth maps with little semantic value.
Endpoint frames do not establish whether the motion is editorially better than a
plain 2D push. A frozen-corpus motion review and the v0.3 core-effect decision
remain open, so this candidate run is not release acceptance.

With Node 22.23.1, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm build`, and `pnpm test` pass (24 unit,
4 integration, 12 depth tests). `pnpm check` reaches `format:check` and stops on
the unrelated untracked `docs/product-positioning.md`; that file was left as found.
