# v0.5 safety and fallback evidence

The `risk-0.5.0` analyzer samples matching source and depth pixels at up to 256 px on the long edge. It records depth range, saturation, edge density, central edge density, and RGB/depth edge disagreement. The score is a versioned safety heuristic, **not** calibrated model confidence. The resolved scene also records overscan shortfall. Scene resolution raises overscan to cover requested travel; the safety pass repairs a shortfall if a scene arrives with one.

Risk below 0.4 leaves motion unchanged. Risk from 0.4 to below 0.8 reduces depth strength, lateral travel, and roll. Strong requests are downgraded to standard or subtle. Flat or extreme depth, risk at least 0.8, or failed depth preparation can produce deterministic 2D pan/zoom with stable reason codes. A neutral depth texture lets the shared WebGL renderer render a 2D fallback without a depth image.

Verification on 2026-09-24:

- `pnpm test:unit`: 36 tests passed, including safe, clamped, flat, extreme, dense-edge, failed-preparation, and crop-envelope cases.
- `pnpm build` and `pnpm lint`: passed.
- `pnpm test:browser:depth`: passed; a flat map produced moving 2D output and the existing depth motion test remained green.
- Candidate corpus review: 43/43 prepared images rendered in the lab without page errors. All 43 remained in depth mode; four had risk-based motion reduction. Risk ranged from 0.024 to 0.781. The candidate review data and source images stay outside version control as private historical evidence.

The 43-image set was retired from Phase 0 selection on 2026-09-25 and was never the frozen benchmark. A new representative corpus and rendered-quality review are required before claiming a release gate.
