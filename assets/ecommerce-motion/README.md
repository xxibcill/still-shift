# Commerce fixtures

sample-one.svg and sample-two.svg are original procedural illustrations created for this implementation. They depict fictional sample containers held by an illustrated hand. Their PNG derivatives are technical fixtures, not real products, product claims, or commercial photography.

Hand and product remain in one raster asset during animation. No retired corpus media is used. The bundled Noto Sans Thai font is distributed with its OFL license and source/checksum record.

beauty-editorial-v1.png was created with the built-in image generation tool for a
more polished 4:5 H03 example. It depicts a fictional SAMPLE 01 cosmetic bottle.
The exact prompt and source/checksum record are saved beside it. The generated
photograph contains only the small product label; headline and CTA are added by
the Commerce renderer. See docs/ecommerce-4x5-example.md.

beauty-cutout-v1.png is the built-in image model's transparent cutout derivative
for H01, H04 and A01. Its alpha is preserved. The bottle and hand stay registered
inside one asset; visible cap and glass details supply the callouts. Its exact
prompt and provenance JSON are saved beside the image.
